# AI Mastery Atlas V11 — Guided Practice & Review

Session recommendations and review actions now open the exact module in the task and evidence desk. Selection is a view choice and never awards progress. The canonical learner-state format, authored curriculum, activities, sources, scoring rubric and retention intervals remain unchanged.

The review queue now supports task/track/repair-text search, review-type filtering, result counts and successive pages beyond the former first-eight limit. Repair, delayed retest, maintenance and transfer priorities remain distinct.

The task desk supports curriculum-task search and selection, navigation to unmet prerequisites, the current-stage authored exercise, acceptance criteria, allowed evidence actions, explicit waiting dates and a bounded view of the existing evidence history. A task switch clears the prior task's unsubmitted assessment fields. Missing prerequisite work cannot be bypassed by selecting a module, and selecting a future retention check does not unlock it early.

Storage-recovery, loading and Share-safe states disable new evidence editing. Accepted evidence is described as accepted in the tab rather than claiming a completed persistent save before the storage writer finishes. No external artifact is inspected automatically.

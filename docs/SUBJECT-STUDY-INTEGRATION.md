# Academic transfer integration — 14 October 2026

The Atlas now includes an academic AI transfer lab at `#subject-study-lab`, plus the Chemistry and Biology Intelligence explorers at `/chemistry-intelligence/` and `/biology-intelligence/`. The existing Physics explorer is linked at its established Performance Powerhouse origin.

The study lab teaches produce → verify → repair → transfer/retest. Its prompt builder prepares five tasks: marked-answer diagnosis, narrow repair, fresh transfer, source-claim audit and source-anchored recall cards. Prompts require the exact question, original unaided answer, source excerpt and marks; absent inputs must be identified before evaluation. AI-authored exercises remain provisional and answers are withheld until attempted. No AI endpoint is invoked and form contents are not persisted or sent over the network by the builder.

Subject explorers use the original 29 September 2026 source-audit JSON recovered from the owner's companion pack. Chemistry preserves 50 mapped 2025 questions and predecessor references. Biology preserves seven year-pair denominators, the 40-topic bank and 15 recent families. Source-ranked forecast interpretation is omitted; corrected primary-unit tags expose the original source label. Original audits remain downloadable unchanged.

Practice records have independent, per-subject browser namespaces with dedicated backups. They are separate from the Atlas learning-state schema and from Powerhouse on its different origin. Import/export supports deliberate manual transfer; no automatic synchronization is claimed. Existing Atlas progress, evaluation and academy namespaces remain unchanged.

Verification includes builds, TypeScript, regression tests, source-count reconciliation, DOM-harness interface checks, share/recovery boundaries, local asset/link validation and internal navigation targets. Real-browser visual and interaction QA was unavailable. The original public audience and production origin are preserved.
